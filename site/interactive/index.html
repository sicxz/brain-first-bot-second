11/16Check

Check the receipts.

Grounded doesn't mean correct.

Receipts are the little numbered citations, like [1], at the end of the notebook's chat answers. Click one and it jumps to the exact sentence in your source. Flashcards and Studio quizzes don't show them, so ask in the chat.

Even with receipts, it can still:
